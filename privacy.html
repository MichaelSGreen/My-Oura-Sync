Privacy Policy

My Oura Sync

Last updated: January 3, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how My Oura Sync ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our service. My Oura Sync is a private integration designed for authorized users to access and sync their Oura health data.

Information Collection and Access

Access to your Oura data occurs only after you explicitly authorize our application through the Oura OAuth authorization process. We aim to request only the minimal scopes needed to provide the service.

When you authorize My Oura Sync, we may access the following types of data from your Oura account:

  • Sleep data and metrics
  • Readiness scores and related metrics
  • Activity data and daily movement
  • Heart rate samples and measurements
  • Workout and session information
  • SpO2 (blood oxygen) measurements
  • Basic profile information and ring configuration

How We Use Your Data

We use your Oura data for the following purposes:

  • Displaying your health metrics and trends in an accessible format
  • Providing data visualizations and summaries
  • Optionally syncing your data to user-controlled systems and platforms that you specify
  • Generating derived insights and summaries based on your data

We do not sell your data to third parties. We do not use your data for advertising purposes.

Data Storage and Retention

We store the following information:

  • OAuth access tokens necessary to retrieve your Oura data
  • Optional cached copies of your metrics and health data
  • Derived summaries and computed values based on your data

We retain your data for the minimum period necessary to provide the service. You may request deletion of your stored data at any time by contacting us at the email address provided in the Contact section below.

Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect your data, including:

  • Limiting access to authorized users only
  • Protecting OAuth tokens with industry-standard security practices
  • Using secure transport protocols where applicable
  • Regularly reviewing our security measures

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data Sharing

We limit data sharing to the following circumstances:

  • With service providers and hosting platforms necessary to operate the service
  • When required by law or legal process
  • To user-controlled systems that you explicitly configure for data synchronization

We do not sell or share your data for marketing or advertising purposes.

Oura Platform and API Data

Oura may collect information related to your use of the Oura platform and API, including usage data associated with this application.

Please refer to Oura's Privacy Policy for information about how Oura collects and uses data.

Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Revoke Access: You may revoke My Oura Sync's access to your Oura data at any time by removing the authorization in your Oura account settings.
  • Request Deletion: You may request deletion of your stored data by contacting us at the email address provided below.
  • Access Your Data: You may request information about what data we have stored by contacting us.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

Contact
